### Reflection

Lessons Learned
I have learned that through the fits and bearing material I have chosen I will have to redesign the shafts. It would be ideal to have the shafts 1.5in in order to properly clear the bearings. Given the very high safety factors of the shafts with their diameters, it is possible to extend them to this length and still resist torsion. The shafts will also need a keyway or flat in order to be adapted to a motor and machine.

This is my first tim using the drawing features in solidworks as well as a part assembly. I can say with 101%confidence I am never using creo again. 

I also changed my design halfway through the CAD designing process. I learned that smaller and lightweight isn't always better. For a single use gearbox a little extra bulk doesn't hurt and makes adaptability easier.

This assignment took me roughly 6 hours to complete.
