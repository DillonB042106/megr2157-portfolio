Jack Stevens
Race car frame design - 
 
Jack’s page is easily navigable using the "Engineering Projects,” tab along with his work, and educational experience. His different projects are listed with a picture and a brief description before clicking on any links. Under each link is the process in which Jack has used to source a problem, recognize a solution, overcome design hurdles, and publish/implement some of his designs.

The majority of Jack's project pages consist of stories of development, and a basic explanation of the design process. No specific measurements, dimensions, or indepth test details are given. Without specific details outside of a brief explanation, it would be nearly impossible to reproduce Jack's work without asking questions.

In the racecar design section of the portfolio, Jack shared a few torsion tests on the frame of the car and explained how the placement of the seat, pedals, and driver display all depended on the torsion and shock experienced in those areas. He also touched on the redesign of the frame on multiple occasions due to failure points due to improper bracing and failed welds. Although the production of the frame had many hurdles, Jack explains the how and why of the crucial change in design to bolster the performance of the car. 

This portfolio definitely uses proper and professional language on all pages to thoroughly explain his processes, experience, and triumphs.
